# NIST Cybersecurity Guidelines

The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) provides a framework for managing cybersecurity risk. It includes best practices for organizations to protect their information systems.

## Core Functions
1. Identify: Understand the assets, data, and risks.
2. Protect: Implement safeguards like access controls and encryption.
3. Detect: Monitor for cybersecurity events.
4. Respond: Take action against detected incidents.
5. Recover: Restore operations after an attack.

## Benefits
- Standardized approach to cybersecurity.
- Helps organizations comply with regulations.
- Reduces risk of data breaches.

## Example
An organization follows NIST guidelines by performing risk assessments, deploying firewalls, and regularly training employees.
